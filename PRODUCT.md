# Product

## Register

product

## Users

South African adults using MyKasiBets inside Telegram, primarily on mobile and
often one-handed. They need to understand the wager, limits, possible return,
result, and updated balance at a glance, including on small screens and unreliable
mobile connections.

## Product Purpose

MyKasiBets provides a trustworthy visual front end for the existing Telegram
betting engine. The Mini App makes games easier to play without moving outcome,
wallet, or settlement authority into the browser. Success means a user can place
one informed wager, see the server-decided result, and reconcile their balance
without ambiguity or duplicate debit risk.

## Brand Personality

Direct, energetic, grounded. The product should feel quick and local without
slang that excludes users or casino spectacle that obscures financial decisions.

## Anti-references

- Neon casino lobbies with flashing jackpots, fake urgency, or confetti overload.
- Generic fintech dashboards with navy-and-gold status cards.
- Game interfaces that animate an outcome before the server confirms it.
- Dense desktop layouts squeezed into a Telegram WebView.

## Design Principles

- Trust before thrill: show stake, limits, result, and balance with no ambiguity.
- One-handed clarity: keep choices large, ordered, and reachable on narrow screens.
- Telegram-native behavior: use native MainButton, BackButton, theme, and haptics
  where supported, with complete fallbacks for older clients.
- Honest motion: animation explains a confirmed result and never predicts one.
- Recover visibly: authentication, network, and balance failures always give a
  clear next action without inventing a game outcome.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Do not rely on color alone for selection or results. Support
reduced motion, keyboard focus, 44px minimum touch targets, system text scaling,
high contrast, and both Telegram light and dark themes.
